A personal assistant for keeping work and life organized.

Personal Chief of Staff is a privately operated AI assistant that helps its owner organize calendar events, prepare for meetings, review tasks, and keep track of commitments. It is not a public sign-up service.

What the assistant does

The assistant uses connected services only to support the owner's requested planning and productivity workflows.

01

Calendar & meetings

Reads Google Calendar events, including time, title, description, and participant details, to create daily schedules and prepare useful talking points before meetings. Calendar changes are made only when expressly requested.

02

Tasks & commitments

Reads Google Tasks to help organize priorities. Separately tracks the owner's commitments and follow-ups in a private personal knowledge base.

03

Daily briefings

Creates concise planning summaries and relevant reminders, delivered through the owner's Telegram bot or accessed through a command-line interface.

How it works

The application operates on a privately managed server. Its primary interface is an AI agent, with a private Obsidian knowledge base for retained notes and commitments.

When the owner authorizes Google access, the assistant uses Google Calendar and Google Tasks data to generate requested or scheduled productivity features. Relevant information may be processed by an AI model provider and included in notifications delivered through Telegram. Selected notes may be synchronized to a private GitHub repository.

Google access is optional and can be revoked through the owner's Google Account. This informational website has no account registration form and does not embed advertising or analytics trackers.
