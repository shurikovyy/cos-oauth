Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Personal Chief of Staff accesses, uses, stores, and shares information, including data obtained through Google APIs.

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Application: Personal Chief of Staff

Contents

1. Overview 2. Google data accessed 3. How data is used 4. Service providers 5. Storage & retention 6. Security 7. Your controls 8. Google Limited Use 9. Updates 10. Contact

1. Overview

Personal Chief of Staff (the “Application”) is a privately operated AI assistant used by its owner for scheduling, meeting preparation, task review, reminders, and managing personal commitments. The Application runs on a privately managed server and is accessed primarily through Telegram or a command-line interface. It is not a public sign-up service.

The operator controls which Google account is connected and which assistant workflows are enabled.

2. Google data accessed

With the account owner's authorization, the Application is designed to access only the Google data needed for its enabled workflows:

  • Google Calendar: calendars and events, including event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, organizer information, participant details, and meeting links where available. Calendar event creation, modification, or deletion may occur when the owner explicitly requests those actions.
  • Google Tasks: task lists and task details, including titles, notes, due dates, and completion status, for reading and planning. The current task integration is read-only.
  • Google OAuth credentials: access and refresh tokens and scope metadata needed to maintain the authorized connection. Google account identity information may be received as part of the consent process.

Scope limitation: The intended Google integration is Calendar and Tasks. Gmail, Google Drive, or other Google data should not be requested unless a corresponding feature is explicitly enabled and this policy is updated beforehand.

3. How data is used

Authorized Google data is used to provide the following user-facing features:

  • Build daily briefings and show upcoming schedule items.
  • Prepare relevant talking points before meetings.
  • Identify deadlines, follow-ups, and potential scheduling conflicts.
  • Provide task summaries and help the owner plan and review work.
  • Carry out calendar changes requested by the owner.
  • Operate, troubleshoot, and secure these features.

Only information considered relevant to a particular task should be included in generated prompts, notifications, or saved notes. The Application does not use Google user data for advertising, marketing targeting, selling data, or evaluating creditworthiness.

4. Service providers and data sharing

To deliver its functions, the Application may send limited relevant information to the following services:

  • Google: handles OAuth authentication and the Calendar and Tasks API requests.
  • OpenAI: processes selected calendar, task, and related context when an AI-generated briefing or response is requested or triggered.
  • Telegram: delivers generated reminders, meeting preparation messages, and other assistant communications to the owner's configured account or chat.
  • GitHub: hosts a private repository used to synchronize the owner's Obsidian notes. Selected summaries or records may be retained in that repository.
  • Server hosting provider: supplies the computing infrastructure on which the Application operates.

These services process information subject to their respective terms and privacy policies. The Application does not sell Google user data or provide it to data brokers, advertising networks, or unrelated third parties. It does not intentionally make private Google Calendar or Tasks content publicly accessible.

5. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are kept in non-public files on the operator's server. Event and task information is normally retrieved as needed. Relevant information may be held temporarily during processing, appear in execution logs or chat histories, or be saved as summaries and commitments in the operator's Obsidian knowledge base. This knowledge base may be synchronized with a private GitHub repository.

Credentials are retained while the connection is active or until removed. Logs and retained notes are kept as needed for continuity and troubleshooting; there is currently no fixed automatic deletion period for every record. The operator may remove stored information and revoke the Google connection. Deleting a local copy does not automatically erase messages or records already processed by external providers, which follow their own retention practices.

This public informational website does not deliberately embed analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking cookies. Its hosting provider may process ordinary technical connection logs.

6. Security

The Application uses OAuth authorization rather than asking for Google account passwords. Access to the server and credentials is restricted to authorized administrators, and service requests use protected network connections where supported. OAuth tokens and private knowledge-base contents are not intentionally published on this website.

As with any online service, absolute security cannot be guaranteed. Relevant safeguards and permissions are reviewed as the Application evolves.

7. Your controls and deletion requests

  • Grant or decline access: Google access is optional and requires authorization through Google's consent flow.
  • Revoke authorization: visit Google Account → Third-party connections and remove access for Personal Chief of Staff.
  • Delete data: contact the operator at the email below to request removal of locally retained Google-derived information and OAuth credentials. For this personal-use deployment, the owner can also directly delete retained notes, histories, and credentials under their control.

Revoking Google authorization prevents future authorized API access but does not by itself delete previously generated messages, notes, or third-party service records.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Personal Chief of Staff's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google data is used only for the application's disclosed, owner-authorized features and necessary operational support. It is not used to serve advertisements, sold to third parties, or used for unrelated profiling. Human access is limited to the operator's own use and authorized troubleshooting, security, or legally required access.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Application's features, connected services, or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published. If a change introduces a new purpose or category of Google data use, applicable disclosures and user consent will be addressed before that use begins.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, access revocation, or deletion requests, contact the application's operator at stove-slept-scope@duck.com.
